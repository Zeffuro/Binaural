Nature photography

These three photographic panoramas come from Poly Haven. They are free public-domain assets under CC0 1.0, including for commercial reuse. Binaural serves its own small WebP versions.

  • Forest slope: Forest Slope by Andreas Mischok. Sunlight through a rocky pine forest.
  • Mountain dawn: Kiara 1 Dawn by Greg Zaal. A mountain valley under a pink and violet dawn.
  • Autumn meadow: Autumn Field by Sergej Majboroda. Long grass beneath a clear blue sky.

The downloadable HDR photographs were tone-mapped to standard display brightness and encoded as 2048 × 1024 WebP panoramas.
