Headphone profiles

SADIE II D1 (KU100 dummy head) and H10 (listener 10) by the University of York AudioLab, from SADIE II v2-2. See Armstrong, Thresh, Murphy and Kearney, A Perceptual Evaluation of Individual and Non-Individual HRTFs: A Case Study of the SADIE II Database (2018).

Measured responses were selected across the sphere, converted from SOFA coordinates to the app's coordinates, and stored as paired floating-point filters with interpolation data. Existing diffuse-field equalisation, ear differences and timing are retained; no independent ear normalisation is applied. These are optional listening choices, not a personalised measurement.

Apache 2.0 license, source and modification notices, profile files and checksums. No endorsement is implied.
