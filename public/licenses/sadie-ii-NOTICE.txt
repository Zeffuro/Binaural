Reference: C. Armstrong, L. Thresh, D. T. Murphy and G. Kearney (2018), A Perceptual Evaluation of Individual and Non-Individual HRTFs: A Case Study of the SADIE II Database, Applied Sciences 8(11), 2029. https://www.mdpi.com/2076-3417/8/11/2029
Changes: selected measured directions, transformed coordinate/ear ordering and stored native 44.1/48kHz paired HRIRs as float32. No normalization, resampling, EQ, time alignment or truncation.
The upstream responses are diffuse-field equalised (paper sections 3.2.2–3.2.3). These 1.2m measurements do not represent near-ear measurement or listener personalization.
The selected source archives contain no separate LICENSE or NOTICE files; the release's separate LICENSE.txt is included unmodified.
